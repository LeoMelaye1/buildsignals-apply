Who they are

A bathroom and roofing remodeler in Southern California.

CaliFirst Remodel serves Los Angeles, Orange County, the Inland Empire and Ventura, with its own sales team running the in-home estimates. They started with us on October 16, 2026. We added roofing ads on November 13.

Step 1

Ads homeowners stop for.

  • Simple cost questions: "Here's what a new walk-in shower should cost you."
  • Tap your city, tap your age: it feels local and made for them.
  • We tested new angles every week and kept the ones that booked appointments, not just the ones that got cheap clicks.
Real ads we ran for CaliFirst on Facebook and Instagram.

Step 2

Only real homeowners get through.

  • Every form asks "Do you own your home?" Answer no, and the form closes.
  • The main bathroom form also asks for the project, the timeline and the street address.
  • Every form ends with a calendar, so homeowners can book their own free in-home estimate.

Step 3

Every lead goes straight to their team.

  • Each lead goes to their sales team the moment the homeowner submits.
  • A text goes out right away, so the homeowner hears back in seconds.
  • Their team calls and books the in-home estimate. Some homeowners book themselves on the calendar.
